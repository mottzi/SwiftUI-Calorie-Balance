# Watch complication design references

Research date: 2026-10-05.

The first additions to Count.kcal answered useful questions, but their graphics were ordinary progress indicators. The user's correction changes the design brief. A large complication should have a recognizable drawing whose shape carries meaning, with text supporting that drawing.

This is a curated set of references, not an all-time ranking. Developer pages establish which graphics are complications. Awards establish recognition of the app, not a separate award for every complication. Official images were inspected directly where noted below. Marketing pages often mix app screens, widgets, notifications, and watch faces, so those images need their labels and context.

## Strongest references

| Reference | Evidence and inspected image | What Count.kcal should learn |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lumy | The developer explicitly describes configurable countdown complications and sun information. Its [Watch page](https://lumy.app/watch/) contains [this Modular face image](https://lumy.app/images/watch/faces/im1.jpg) and a [face collection](https://lumy.app/images/watch/faces/faces_hero.jpg). These are whole faces containing Lumy complications, not third-party replacements for the watch face. | The solar drawing gives the large slot a distinct identity. The event, time, and position belong to one picture. This is the strongest reference for a restrained, custom calorie graphic. |
| Weathergraph | The developer confirms large graph complications showing upcoming temperature, rain, and wind. Its [face collection](https://weathergraph.app/watchfaces) identifies the forecast chart on [OutdoorKit](https://weathergraph.app/watchfaces/weathergraph-outdoorkit-500x500.png). The image is a whole face with several apps; the center weather chart is the Weathergraph component. The [current complication guide](https://weathergraph.app/guides/apple-watch-weather-complications) distinguishes face complications, Smart Stack widgets, and the full app. | A wide slot can carry an actual time story. Layered lines, bars, and a small set of labels communicate changing conditions. A Count.kcal drawing should expose when energy balance changed, not only its latest percentage. |
| Watchsmith | David Smith's [2021 release article](https://www.david-smith.org/blog/2021/04/08/watchsmith-2-0/) explicitly calls [Solar Path](https://www.david-smith.org/resources/SolarPath%26Time.png) a complication for Modular faces. The image is a rendered complication component. One broad curve crosses a horizon, a hollow marker locates the present, and one label names the next transition. [Modern Moonphase](https://www.david-smith.org/resources/ModernMoonphase.png), [Moon & Stars](https://www.david-smith.org/resources/Moon%26Stars.png), and [Geometric Solar](https://www.david-smith.org/resources/PlainGeo-Solar.png) are also documented complication styles. All four images were inspected directly. | Solar Path is exceptionally economical. A few custom paths and one marker make the data visible without surrounding controls. The moon examples show that texture, a silhouette, and deliberate empty space can make a complication feel like an object. Geometric Solar shows how data can become an abstract instrument. These are historical design references, not a claim that the 2021 rendering is unchanged today. |
| CARROT Weather | Apple named it [2021 Apple Watch App of the Year](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2021/12/app-store-awards-honor-the-best-apps-and-games-of-2021/) and gave it a [2021 Interaction Design Award](https://developer.apple.com/design/awards/2021/). The developer's [v5 design account](https://www.meetcarrot.com/weather/v5.html) says its weather icons were drawn for even the smallest Watch complication, with precipitation quantity expressed through droplet fill. Its [2019 release notes](https://support.meetcarrot.com/weather/css/whatsnew-mobile.html) confirm 5-hour and 10-hour large Infograph Modular charts, with precipitation bars behind the 5-hour chart. The exact chart options are historical. A current chart screenshot was not visually verified in this research. | Draw the symbol for this particular size and meaning. Changing the interior of a meaningful symbol communicates more than placing another stock symbol beside a percentage. The chart also puts related measurements in the same coordinate system. |
| Heart Analyzer | The developer's [Advanced Apple Watch Complications section](https://www.helixapps.co.uk/heart-analyzer) contains an [official face collection](https://images.squarespace-cdn.com/content/v1/5ea5565e8a60162dcfc27a78/800f2aff-ffc9-4c0d-b74f-c7402353a60c/Monday%2C%2B18%2BAug%2B2025%2B11%3A10%3A49.png). The inspected image shows compact heart-rate charts within actual watch faces. Its [App Store description](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/heart-analyzer-pulse-tracker/id1006420410?platform=watch) confirms customizable complications for several heart metrics. | A dominant curve and a small amount of context make a medical-style instrument legible. This is a useful information hierarchy reference, though Lumy and Watchsmith offer a stronger visual personality. |

## Directions for Count.kcal

These are design proposals inferred from the references. They have not been implemented or validated.

1. **Energy contour.** Give the large rectangle to a custom drawing of cumulative intake and expenditure through the day. Meals create honest steps in intake; expenditure creates a separate line. The space between them expresses balance. Mark the present with a small point, distinguish any midnight estimate with a dashed continuation, and keep one primary value. This is my first choice. It uses Count.kcal's actual subject to produce a distinctive silhouette and follows the useful time story in Weathergraph and Solar Path.
2. **Balance instrument.** Draw a broad, finely marked horizontal scale with a custom pointer or suspended balance. The center represents the user's balance goal, and the pointer shows the current or midnight estimate. Its geometry must distinguish remaining allowance from energy balance. This is closer to Watchsmith's abstract mechanical drawings and could remain clear in a monochrome face.
3. **Meal fingerprint.** Render today's real intake as a compact row of individually drawn food-event shapes, sized by energy and placed by time. One small total gives the exact number. This would make each day visually distinct, but it requires reliable event timestamps and clear aggregation rules. Avoid inventing recognizable food illustrations when the recorded data contains only nutrients.

The next design should start with one large rectangular complication. Evaluate the drawing at its actual face size before expanding it into circular variants. Keep the existing rings as functional options if useful, but they should not determine the new visual language. Any forecast must look different from recorded data; custom artwork should explain the underlying data rather than disguise it.

Count.kcal Watch — 12 additional framed assets
Created 18 October 2026

These are transparent PNG compositions of current Count.kcal Watch Midnight screens inside original Apple Watch product-frame PNGs. They use the largest bezel size for each model: Series 11 46 mm, Ultra 2 49 mm, and Ultra 3 49 mm. The full watch, case, and band remain visible; no frame is reused from the prior set.

All app screens come from the current simulator captures in Marketing/2026-10-watch/captures. Those captures use Dark appearance, the Dark background setting, Marketing Mode, Steps off, and Midnight mode. Theme 7 (the grayscale black-and-white palette) is excluded. The selected palettes are Themes 1, 2, 4, 5, 8, and 9.

The required Apple Watch Ultra 2 49 mm Black titanium with Trail Loop Black is included as:
apple-watch-ultra-2-49mm-black-titanium-trail-loop-black-theme-02-dark-midnight-bg-dark-steps-off-framed.png

No image uses the earlier Apple Watch Series 11 46 mm Aluminum Jet Black with Black Sport Band frame. The Series 11 variants use other case finishes and bands; the Ultra variants include Alpine Loop, Milanese Loop, Ocean Band, and Trail Loop combinations.

Frame screen apertures are matched to the Apple PNGs: Series 11 46 mm is 416 x 496 px, Ultra 2 49 mm is 410 x 502 px, and Ultra 3 49 mm is 422 x 514 px. The existing 416 x 496 simulator capture is resampled only as needed to fill the Ultra apertures; it is not cropped. Each output uses the full Apple frame at its supplied resolution: 560 x 880 px for Series 11, 600 x 940 px for Ultra 2, or 600 x 960 px for Ultra 3.

Files:
- Twelve individual transparent PNGs, one for each watch/band/theme combination.
- apple-watch-additional-frames-manifest.csv lists model, finish, band, app theme, source capture, and output resolution.

Apple Watch frame source files are from Apple Design Resources. Demo data only; no personal HealthKit data is shown.

Original Apple frame PNGs are retained locally in ../../source/frames/.
The compositor no longer depends on mounted Apple disk images.

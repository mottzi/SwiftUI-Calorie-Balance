Count.kcal: Make it yours

A 12-second website loop about color customization across iPhone and Watch.
The silver iPhone 18 Pro and black Apple Watch Series 11 reveal four matching
dark app palettes. Each device gives a small upward bounce as the color wipe
reaches it, then settles with a soft rebound. The film returns to its opening
state for a seamless loop.

DELIVERABLES

videos/count-kcal-make-it-yours-loop-transparent.webm
  1800 x 1000, 30 fps, 12 seconds, VP9 with alpha, silent.
  Transparent outside the artwork. White copy is intended for a dark site.

videos/count-kcal-make-it-yours-loop-dark-bg.mp4
  Same composition and timing, H.264, with a fixed #0c1016 background.
  Use for review, sharing, or an opaque video placement.

stills/count-kcal-make-it-yours-poster-transparent.png
  3600 x 2000 transparent poster at 1.6 seconds.

source/make-it-yours.html
  Editable Canvas artwork and interactive preview. Open in a browser.
  It links the existing Today and Watch PNGs, so keep those collections at
  their current relative paths. No duplicated screenshots are included here.
  Palette buttons pause on each theme. The slider scrubs the film. Play
  resumes the loop. Reduced-motion preference starts the preview paused.
  Add ?t=4&paused=1 to inspect theme 02, or ?t=2.94&paused=1 for the bounce.

SEQUENCE

0.0 to 2.0 s      Blue and orange, theme 01.
2.0 to 3.4 s      Reveal indigo and green, theme 02, with a device bounce.
3.4 to 4.9 s      Hold theme 02.
4.9 to 6.3 s      Reveal purple and gold, theme 08, with a device bounce.
6.3 to 7.8 s      Hold theme 08.
7.8 to 9.2 s      Reveal teal and coral, theme 09, with a device bounce.
9.2 to 10.4 s     Hold theme 09.
10.4 to 11.8 s    Return to theme 01 with a device bounce.
11.8 to 12.0 s    Settle into the opening state, ready to loop.

All four palettes use dark appearance. Each bounce lasts 0.64 seconds.
The iPhone lifts 18 px and the Watch 22 px at the 1800 x 1000 export size,
with a small scale pulse and tilt. The Watch starts slightly later to follow
where the reveal reaches its screen. Both settle before the loop boundary.

All phone screenshots use Marketing Mode, Midnight, dark background setting,
Steps off and the captured streak highlight. Watch screenshots use Marketing
Mode and Midnight. Both devices stay in dark appearance throughout.
The film uses the existing captures without repainting their interface.
The wipe is a marketing transition, not a recording of an in-app theme switch.
Original screenshot clocks and data remain as captured.

WEBSITE USE

Set video playback to muted, playsinline and loop. Provide a pause control
and show the poster instead of autoplay for reduced-motion users.
The WebM contains alpha, but validate alpha playback in your target browsers.
The MP4 has a fixed background. The HTML source offers a Canvas alternative.
This is a website composition, not an App Store submission export.

SOURCE NOTES

The HTML contains the image references, timing, easing and complete layout.
Exports were rendered with CoreGraphics using system fonts, then encoded with
FFmpeg. Video is 1800 x 1000 to keep the transparent loop practical for web.
The poster is 2x resolution. The repo is the sole asset location.

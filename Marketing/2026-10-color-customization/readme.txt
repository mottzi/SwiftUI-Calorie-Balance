The flower contains twelve original silver iPhone 18 Pro framed screenshots.
Themes 01, 02, 04, 05, 08 and 09 each appear once in light mode and once in dark
mode. Every screen uses Marketing Mode, Midnight, the dark background setting,
Steps off and the streak highlight captured mid-sweep.

theme positions. Each of the six themes appears twice in the chosen appearance.

The screens and hardware come directly from 2026-10-today/stills. They were
scaled and rotated as complete images. No screen content was generated,
retouched or replaced in the final asset.

Composition
  Twelve phones, 30 degrees apart with a 15-degree offset. The mixed variant
  alternates light and dark. Phone height 1850 px. Their top edges are 1820 px from the square
  center before rotation. Subtle contact shadows separate overlapping frames.

Brief
  Arrange the existing framed theme variants into a square flower-like fan
  with a transparent background. Preserve the real app screens and keep all
  hardware inside the canvas. Use six palettes in both light and dark mode.

A built-in image-generation draft was rejected because it altered the UI.
The delivered image is a deterministic composition of the original assets.
No production app code was changed for this asset.

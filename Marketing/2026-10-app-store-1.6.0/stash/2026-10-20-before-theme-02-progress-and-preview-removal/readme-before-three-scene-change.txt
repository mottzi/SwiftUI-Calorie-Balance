Scope

Composition status

medium and large exports. These were uploaded to the 1.6.0 draft. The owner
rejected their composition as weaker than the original set. Their draft
assignments have been replaced by v2. They remain here as work history.

flat-angle experiments are kept outside final/ and are not production assets.
All 16 final screenshots are uploaded and saved in the 1.6.0 draft, four per
locale in the documented order.

All four v2 videos are uploaded and assigned to the draft. Local full-decode
and technical checks passed for every export. Apple's browser preview had
not yet displayed the new video poster when the final media check was made.
